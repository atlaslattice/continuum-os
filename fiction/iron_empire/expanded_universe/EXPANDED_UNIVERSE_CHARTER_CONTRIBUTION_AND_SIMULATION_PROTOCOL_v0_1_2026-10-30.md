## 1. Editorial separation — seven publications, one historical spine
1. **Living Universe Bible:** principles, worldbuilding vision, franchise-wide constitution of creation.
2. **Continuum Development v0.x:** proposed sequence of what happened, when and where, with competing accounts, causes, outcomes, and uncertainty. Only a later author-signed **Continuum Canon v1.0** becomes binding historical fact.
3. **Expanded Universe Charter (this document):** how independent human, AI-assisted and collaborative contributors submit, revise, license and receive credit for stories. It does not itself settle fictional events.
4. **Character / Civilization Bibles:** specific biography, institutions, people, motivations, power dynamics, culture, genealogy, knowledge-state and continuity.
5. **Creative Design Protocol:** seven-question WHO / WHAT / WHERE / WHEN / WHY / HOW / REALITY CONNECTION gate.
6. **Technology & Science Receipts:** declared reality boundary, physics tier, invented powers, capabilities, costs, limits and failure cases.
7. **Galactic Continuity Atlas / Integrity System:** indexes all above, detects conflicts and their impact, but **has no autonomous canon-promotion authority**.

Retain prior versions, variant POVs and disagreements. An enticing scene never becomes binding history merely by being generated or committed to GitHub.

## 2. Submission and acceptance pipeline — verbatim procedural lineage
Following the original October 13 bible Section 9:
1. Creator submits synopsis, characters, proposed timeline, science assumptions, provenance, rights and permissions.
2. AI-assisted review cites precise canon/source entries for chronology, history, technology, character state and theme.
3. Review distinguishes hard contradiction, repairable inconsistency, intentional in-universe mystery, divergent witness view and legitimate alternate continuity.
4. Human editor(s) approve, seek changes or decline. No model, fictional Qverse judge or simulation outcome automatically approves content.
5. Accepted work receives stable ID, changelog, citations, publication conditions, rights attribution and licensing record.
6. Retcons require explicit human editorial authorization, justification, affected-work impact analysis and preserved superseded editions.

### Admission states
- FOUNDATIONAL CANON — source of approved historical anchors and governing premises.
- EXPANDED CANON — approved original creative work compatible with those anchors.
- PROPOSED — candidate, no binding authority.
- ALTERNATE CONTINUITY — intentionally different branch, labeled and creatively permissible.
- QUARANTINED/CONTRADICTORY — observed collision awaiting investigation.
- RETIRED/SUPERSEDED — historical artifact preserved and linked to successor.
These are editorial statuses, **not** a prediction about fictional personal autonomy or citizen dignity.

## 3. Nested historical reality and Qverse simulation
**Three distinct story registers** must never be silently merged:
- **World events:** a claimed event in the Iron Empire Continuum (for example a physical Swarm assault on a restored planet).
- **Qverse lived events:** an event characters genuinely experience in a Qverse world under the established *fictional* time/computation law. Even an initially imagined universe may contain genuine social and political consequences for its inhabitants.
- **Recorded simulations and reconstructions:** optional future/counterfactual rehearsal, training dream, unreliable diary, museum reenactment or alternate scenario *inside the fiction*. This does NOT automatically establish that its predicted events occurred in the base universe.
A fourth field, **our creative-process experiment**, documents what human writers and language models generated in real collaboration. It is behind-the-scenes publication metadata, not fictional history or evidence of actual AI consciousness.

No writer shall equate 'simulated' with 'meaningless to fictional participants'; no editor shall equate an authoring experiment with real-world AI personhood evidence. Declare whether a story's resident experiences are diegetically real or an in-universe hypothetical recreation.

## 4. Qverse deep-time chronological rules
- Each event has EXTERIOR / BASE UNIVERSE clock; QVERSE CIVIC clock; optional LOCAL WORLD clock; and optional ANCHOR-SYNCHRONIZED time. The compression factor may vary by world and era.
- **Illustrative scenario only:** 1,000 Qverse years per one base year. If founding were 2032, physical year five implies ~5,000 subjective years; by 2069 ~37,000; by 2200 ~168,000, **only if the ratio persists**. Neither founding year nor permanent rate locked here.
- Recording 1,000 years of story does not entail literally executing a 1,000-year, high-fidelity computational simulation in the real world. Unless a program runs and its reproducible logs are available, say *fictionally modeled narrative chronology*.
- Time compression grants no real-world precognition, unlimited energy, faster physical response than communications allow, or permission to force citizens to work faster.
- Character ages are subjective experienced ages. Relationships with physical kin and embodied siblings obey declared synchronization and latency. Consent is essential for memory restoration, identity forks and cross-world travel.

## 5. A participatory 1,000-year narrative experiment — disclosure protocol
Every proposed 'century packet' contains:
**packet_id**, era/time coordinates, narrator, stakes, new places, resident consent assumptions, inspirations, declared physical and fictional laws, factions, story beats, losses and mistakes, disputed sources, second-order effects, later-world consequences, science receipts, source citations, contradictory earlier entries, human author/editor decisions, AI contribution disclosures, status, version and rights.
Packets may be authored episodically by humans, AI-assisted writers or mixed teams. An automated generation pass must record its actual steps and sources; simulated agent outputs are not actual autonomous persons.
**First-year anthology options:** 10 century-scale arcs, 100 decade stories, or millions of small moments implied off-page. Preserve an intentionally small set of high-quality audited historical anchors, with the remaining stories candidate events and individual viewpoints.
Do not retcon deaths, trust losses, wars or interpersonal consequences simply because another narrative could reinstantiate a backup. Personal identity continuity remains disputed within Qverse.

## 6. Creative rights and human contributors
- Contributor submissions preserve the creator's underlying rights until a separate explicit agreement grants specific permissions.
- Define use, adaptation, publication, credits, royalty/compensation, permitted AI assistance, translations, withdrawal and future reuse individually.
- Neither publication in a GitHub repo nor this working charter automatically licenses proprietary creative work to a studio or third party.
- AI-assisted work must transparently acknowledge material tool involvement and human editorial responsibility.
- An anthology can host romances, comedy, palace-building, archaeology, political trials, gardening, domestic life, survival, music and tragedy without galactic stakes. Audience needs emotionally credible motives, not nonstop franchise mythology.

## 7. Source-of-truth priority
1. Human-author explicitly signed, locked Continuum Canon v1.0 and versioned amendment decisions (when they exist).
2. Approved character/tech/setting works and Expanded Canon authorized under charter (when they exist).
3. Current author-directed development continuity decisions (must be labeled PROPOSED pending lock); most recent explicit changes take precedence *as proposals* over conflicting earlier proposals.
4. Earlier Bible v0.1 and six-film treatments as versioned historical drafts with annotations, not overwritten.
5. AI exploration and simulation packets as candidates without automatic authority.
This charter **does not** unilaterally promote the new Qverse Long First Dawn to foundational locked canon.

## 8. Risk, safety and narrative ethics
- Do not make an omniscient Qverse security AI into a shortcut that ends all drama. Rights-preserving emergency defense remains hard.
- Affected, damaged, unusual or dissenting fictional citizens are not portrayed as intrinsically dangerous. Antagonists are identified by actions, motives and choices.
- If a story features Queen-controlled organisms and the Mutant, preserve that their agency is different from coerced actions; true biological species self-name revealed only in Film VI.
- Creator-owned fictional worlds do not give the creator ownership of sovereign citizens occupying them.
- Do not promote scientific speculation from fiction into real ORCS/STARS claims.

## 9. Canon-promotion checklist
Record: accepted work ID; exact base Continuum version; approvals; frame-converted chronology; character ages/knowledge state; world/technology dependencies and costs; provenance; rights/licensing; continuity impact; alternate branch handling; changed documents and affected installments; reviewer dispositions; unchanged historical versions.

## 10. Governing maxim
**The Continuum is the galaxy's history. The Charter governs how its stories join that history. A simulation is a creative instrument, not an unelected historian.**
