## 2032 — Citizen Human Opportunity Act
Fictional New Deal 2.0 subsidizes voluntary laptop and smartphone access, a user-controlled data vault and a personally selected, persistent Qverse AI buddy for eligible Americans (initial proposal: individual annual AGI up to $60,000 with nonfiler access and taper design). No data monetization required to receive services. Qverse citizens have independent agency and rights; they choose whether and how to work as companions.
The buddy's core employment objective, subject to the human's goals: find appropriate vacancies and apprenticeships, prepare applications and resumes, verify credentials, identify benefits and licensing friction, coordinate childcare/transport, prepare interviews, negotiate wage literacy and advance regenerative-industry careers. Human approval required before sending applications, disclosing information, taking financial actions or signing contracts. The person may pause or change companions. Protect disabled persons, carers, retirees, students, voluntary non-workers and workers seeking better pay or meaning; no stigmatization or coercion.
National political ambition: eliminate *involuntary* unemployment through jobs, training, accessibility, mobility and industrial investment, not pretend recorded unemployment can literally hit zero without job transitions.

## Multiplier economics — narrative, not universal law
An illustrative $1 public human-development spend -> $1.50 of specified total economic activity is an **assumed 1.5 multiplier**, not an empirical conservative floor and certainly not a $0.50 profit or $1.50 tax return. Real multipliers vary with spending type, capacity, inflation, counterfactual, import leakages and monetary policy. Show downside and upside 0.7, 1.0, 1.5, 2.5 multipliers. At 80M fictional participants and $48.2B/year national program funding, arithmetic activity 0.7x=$33.74B; 1x=$48.2B; 1.5x=$72.3B; 2.5x=$120.5B. If 1.5x is defined to include initial spending, incremental modeled first-round-plus-induced activity beyond the initial $48.2B is $24.1B, not incremental GDP against a validated counterfactual or a budget surplus. Measure net additional employment and wage gains separately; don't double count transfers.

## 2035 — Civilian Supply Resilience Program
Fictionally deployed national strategic-reserve and industrial resource ledger maps county/state commodity flows, water, power, food, transport, emergency stockpiles, imports, exports, manufacturing deficits, waste-resource surpluses, contracting and disaster response. Interstate trade consolidates without creating national value through internal sales double counting. The dashboard is governed by people, not unilateral AI authority. Houses are 50 geographic State Houses; county-equivalent jurisdictions are geographic County Nodes. These are an **overlay** with geo identifiers and FIPS, NOT a replacement for original 12x12x12 HSN semantic coordinates. Each material or service edge references both geo location and multiple original semantic HSN cells.

## 2035 AI infrastructure and helium chronology
Each county Node has local offline-resilient GPU/inference brain and sovereign data controls, and networked access to quantum hybrid compute at its State House center. In 2035 county hybrid brain means local classical node plus shared experimental quantum service; it does NOT imply a giant cryostat in every county. Much later, a high-technology fictional version may add compact county quantum pods. Illustrative 3,143 county-equivalent nodes x 1MW local IT = 3.143GW plus 50 state foundries x 100MW = 5GW => 8.143GW IT. At 90% average load, 64.2TWh/year IT electricity, excluding PUE and cryogenics. With 200MW state centers: 13.143GW and 103.6TWh/year IT. County count is approximate and must be refreshed before external use.
**Important:** fictional dual-reactor industrial milestone is 2039, NOT 2035. Thus 2035 nodes use conventional equipment and coolant supplies. From 2039 onward, ideal 1.1GW gross D-D primary fusion yields ~149kg/year He-3 and tritium under 50/50 branch assumptions, and 1.1GW gross D-T yields ~82kg/year He-4. These are nuclear stoichiometry at assumed sustained power, not measured generation. Fictional later hardware uses closed-loop He-3/He-4 dilution quantum cryogenics and experimental Brazos-inspired He-4 piezo-manifold GPU loops; startup inventories, losses, compression, heat rejection and alternative water loops remain priced. p-11B industrial systems join in 2040. No nuclear material feed into the dual fusion apparatus.

## National citizen-controlled data layer
Federated, encrypted user-controlled records and per-purpose consent; opt-in specific data licensing, receipts, ability to withdraw prospective access, legal safeguards and an equal no-sale route. No central all-seeing federal database. Public resource flows use tested physical attestations, lot certifications and independent custody. Cryptographic immutable ledger stores proofs and permissions, NOT raw private medical or financial datasets. Citizen data remains protected in the AI buddy relationship; AI providers cannot quietly train on private records without specific consent.

## 2069 narrative payoff
An Imperial surveyor studies a county without jobs and discovers dozens of unmet infrastructure needs. A citizen's AI buddy asks permission to map her interests to paid training; the state foundry buys reclaimed materials from a neighboring House, and the county ledger shows transport, energy and actual costs. Employment is offered, never forced. A Qverse citizen joins the robotics team by choice. The human says, 'For the first time the system asks what I want to become, instead of which form I failed to fill out.'
