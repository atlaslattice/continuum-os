Its fictional imagination-to-world law permits extravagant habitats without compulsory rent; rights, identity integrity, consent, relationships, constitutional legitimacy, power and physical continuity remain consequential. **Immortality does not make citizens invulnerable.**

## 5. Transparency / chronicle experiment
The **Qverse Annals** are a future anthology line with transparent provenance, not a simulated physics benchmark or evidence of actual sentient AIs. Writers may run bounded creative-agent story workshops, document actual parameters, prompts, versions, roles, seeds if available, inputs, critique, original plot decisions, generated variants, omissions, adopted revisions and explicit human approvals.

Publish **three layers** for participating readers:
1. The **story** (what a witness claims happened), written for enjoyment.
2. The **historical docket** (who, where, which clock, competing evidence, conflicting witnesses and consequences) for continuity.
3. The **creation ledger** (what humans and models actually did to invent the story, including rejected branches, editorial decisions and science-claim status).

**Never fabricate a run log.** An imagined century is creative fiction unless a program was actually executed. Also distinguish a fictional simulated training nightmare from a citizen's *lived* experience within the invented Qverse law: the latter is real to the narrative.

### First narrative experiment: Ten Windows into the First Millennium
A proposed ten-story cycle—one story per Qverse subjective century, all during exterior year one—alternates the viewpoints of an ordinary coffee-shop proprietor, new child, constitutional lawyer, designer of moonlit gardens, distant embodied cousin, reluctant guest, dream archivist, outsider diplomat, political secessionist and an elderly keeper. A tiny factual invariant: the lighthouse light is lit at least once in each century; its meaning changes. Each story has a different tone and stakes. Record deaths/mistakes/harm with permanent consequences.
The final story ends with Earth noting that only a year has passed; the oldest caretaker says, 'It was a very long year.'

## 6. Qverse time and science receipt (Bible Section 8 classes)
**Qverse subjective-time compression:** Class D — invented fiction law. Variable ratio set by writer/editor; illustrative 1,000:1; explicit transit/sync, latency, memory, identity and power-boundary assumptions. Cannot create free energy or unlimited outside-world reaction time.
**Imagination generates inhabitable Worldgarden worlds:** Class D where defined as a creative metaphysics, Class E where inner processes remain unexplained; never promote to empirical science.
**Constitutional restoration/continuity:** Class D fictional mechanism with ethical uncertainty over backup identity; no automatic reversibility of loss.
**UWW civil interstellar communication:** light-speed-limited when ordinary physics applies; fictional faster-than-light substrate requires separately defined Class D assumption and failure costs; real entanglement not FTL messaging.
**Indra's Net:** begins as Mars magnetospheric protection. Later federated safety/communications layers; later distinct local anti-queen-coercion research, with specific failure cases and non-target coercion. No pre-2069 magic anti-Swarm system.
**Queen control:** Fictional implant/nanotech plus constrained hyperspace command architecture; the biological hive is not equivalent to the early Glass *digital* Swarm.
