## IMPORTANT carbon and economic counting corrections
Total government gross outlay != net societal cost. Recovered material sales, water revenues and paid compute service can offset some public cost if legally earmarked; revenues are not guaranteed and may belong to private operators. Internal industrial transactions across state Houses cancel in national GDP accounting. Human 1.5× multiplier is not a verified economic floor and cannot offset government budget dollar-for-dollar: economic activity, tax receipts and fiscal savings are different.
Possible annual *fictional* external sales reference: $30B foundry industrial output (50×$600M), desal at 90% output ~6.217 billion m3 × $1–3/m3 = ~$6.2–18.7B gross; state data-center inference services could be sold but citizen internal services are not counted twice. Subtract customer payments and external sales only in cash flow for the actual funder/operator, not by simply claiming program self-financing.
