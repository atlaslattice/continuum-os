### III. NATIONAL SLUDGE — actual US sources and fictional routes
**Units critical**: dry metric tonnes biosolids differ from wet tonnes sludge and sewage water volume; biosolids, drinking-water sludges, desal coagulant solids and brine salts are distinct and MUST NOT be added as identical dry biomass.

Do not assume 4M is all domestic sludge or add extrapolated alternate total on top.
A broader national dry biosolids planning scenario can use **7M dry metric tonnes/year**, explicitly NOT a verified 2026 census, rather than the EPA reporting subset; use separate scenario toggles.
Potential wet sludge fuel: source-separated biodegradable organics and sewage sludge via **anaerobic digestion** into biogas/biomethane; wet sludge via **hydrothermal liquefaction** into biocrude as research/deployment scenario; thermochemical conversion of dried, contaminant-screened residues where appropriate. Recover wastewater nutrients (struvite), suitable industrial heat and treated reuse water; pathogens, PFAS, metals and microplastics require measured removal and permanent handling. Organic carbon converted to gas or oil cannot also be claimed as biochar or fertilizer. EPA biosolids has land-use pathways already; don't call all 4M an entirely new energy source.
