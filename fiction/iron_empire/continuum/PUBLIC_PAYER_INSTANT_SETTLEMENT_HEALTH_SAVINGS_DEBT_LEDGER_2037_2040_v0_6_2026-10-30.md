## Fictional 2037 government public insurer
By 2037 United States launches publicly operated comprehensive direct-pay coverage, with insurance funded by consolidated existing federal/state public funds, replacement household/employer healthcare contributions through explicit legislation, transparent premiums or taxes, and reserve rules. Every person within eligibility receives medically necessary evidence-based covered care (including drugs, dental, vision, rehabilitation, mental health, disability supports and home care) with no adversarial surprise coverage exclusions. Retain medical judgement, quality checks, fraud control, informed consent, access, clinical capacity and appeals. 2040: duplicative private insurance loses its business role in the fictional economy; transition staff redeployment, provider reliability and continuity of care require transition budgets.
Providers submit standardized invoices with validated clinical service receipts. Contractually valid, low-risk items settle in seconds or minutes through compliant dollar-denominated payment rails, with a privacy-preserving cryptographic receipt; complex or suspicious claims require human/clinical review before payment. **Not all medical payments can be instant final and irreversible**: avoid paying fraudulent or invalid claims automatically. Blockchain has hashes, permission/provenance proofs, reconciliation and payment audit, never raw diagnosis records; medical data stays encrypted under regulated access.

## Four-ledger accounting
L1 **Current funds redirected:** employer premiums, household premiums, Medicare/Medicaid budget, state contributions, etc. Migrating funds to public insurer increases public receipts AND public claims by comparable amounts, not free money.
L2 **Clinical payouts:** hospital, doctor, pharmaceutical, dental and long-term care costs, administrative residual, utilization change, inflation, reserves; publicly negotiated sustainable provider rates.
L3 **True resource savings:** claims/billing simplification, lower procurement/pharma prices, less denials/rework, better coordinated prevention, lower fraud and a credible measured reference spending path; subtract new care, compliance, cybersecurity, transition and displaced-worker costs.
L4 **Treasury-distributable surplus:** actual legally designated public fund cash after L2, reserves, stabilizers and commitments. Only L4 can help pay New Deal 2.0 debt, or a separate legislated Treasury transfer when separately budgeted. A spending reduction that accrues to households isn't automatically Treasury revenue; capture and distribution require actual tax/financing rules.
