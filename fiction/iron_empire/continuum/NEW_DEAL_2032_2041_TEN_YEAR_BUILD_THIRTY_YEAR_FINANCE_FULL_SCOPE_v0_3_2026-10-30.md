## Time and financing
BUILD start 2032, finish 2041 (10 distinct fiscal-year cohorts). Each year's capital tranche ~$267.84B (flat for illustrative scenario, actual build will be nonuniform) is financed with 30-year fixed 2% amortizing financing from issuance, not a 100-year note. Annual annuity factor .02/(1-1.02^-30) = 0.0446499223.
Per annual cohort debt service ~$11.959B/year for 30 years. In construction year 1 there is ~$11.96B debt service; construction year 10 ~$119.59B/year as cohorts stack; average debt service over construction years ~$65.77B/y, sum of first 10 years' annual debt service ~$657.75B. Full set of annual tranches are repaid sequentially in 2061–2070, assuming issue years 2032–2041, and no refinancing. Total original financed cohort payments (30 years each, not ten) **$3.5877T**, including **$909.3B interest** on $2.6784T borrowed principal. Total is undiscounted nominal-at-2% arithmetic with costs assumed in constant 2026 dollars (a stylized treatment; actual nominal and real rates cannot be conflated).
Direct 10-year gross construction capital spending of ~$2.678T is FINANCED by borrowing. Do not **add construction capex and principal loan repayments** when computing consolidated lifetime resource costs; debt payment is a fiscal cash-flow presentation, construction spending is an asset/resource-cost presentation.
Annual spending in fully scaled operation: prior package $149.94B annual operations/refresh plus new allowance: housing operations 20B, water/wastewater 12B, grid 8B, added clean energy 12B, freight 10B, security 5B, workforce ongoing services 15B = **$231.94B/year**. Prior baseline $149.94B already included $13B/year device replacements, $25B/year compute replacement allowance, other IT and energy OPEX and original foundry/desal OPEX. Distinguish refresh sinking funds from subsequent new loans to prevent duplication.
After construction, while all ten loans are outstanding, annual fictional gross cash need = **$231.94B + $119.59B = ~$351.53B/y**, before verified revenue. During ten-year construction, if operations ramp linearly 10%→100% and loans issued equally yearly: mean OPEX ~$127.57B/y and mean debt service ~$65.77B/y, approximate annual average cash ~$193.34B/y, cumulative initial-decade financed cash ~$1.933T, excluding separately financed capex proceeds; assumes immediate same-year debt service and steady real costs. This is a pedagogical ramp case not a forecast. Full debt schedule declines after 2061 as annual cohorts mature. Unspent construction contingency is not automatic operating cash.

## Fiscal/social net value methodology
Budget outlay ≠ incremental GDP ≠ dividend ≠ profit. No automatic credit for 1.5x human investment multiplier as federal receipts. Water/material sales by state enterprises have real counterparties; payments between Houses cancel in a national consolidated revenue account. Avoided imports count actual displaced external purchases net of domestic costs/foreign exports foregone; CO2/ecological restoration separate physical metrics and use explicit shadow-price scenarios with no double counting. Each state House and county node annually closes power, water, material, labor, freight, debt and privacy-licensing ledgers.
