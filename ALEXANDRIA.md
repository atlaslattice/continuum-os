# Alexandria 2.0 Beta

Alexandria 2.0 is the navigation and knowledge-graph layer for the public Atlas Lattice corpus.

The source repositories remain independent, forkable, attributable, and historically intact. Alexandria makes their relationships computable and their public meaning legible.

## Start in five minutes

### 1. Understand the architecture

Read `atlas-lattice-foundation` for the platform architecture, 144-Sphere ontology, regenerative infrastructure concepts, and canonical specifications.

### 2. Understand the evidence discipline

Read [CANON.md](CANON.md). Alexandria distinguishes measured, derived, simulated, proposed, hypothetical, historical, reference, and quarantined material.

### 4. Explore the Library

The wider `atlaslattice` GitHub corpus contains restored upstream/reference repositories. These are Alexandria's public library stacks: useful source material with original licenses, authorship, and histories preserved. They are not automatically Atlas-authored canon.

## Hypermatrix principle

The 12 Houses × 12 Spheres lattice is a projection over structured relationships, not a requirement that every artifact live in exactly one directory.

An artifact can simultaneously relate to multiple dimensions such as:

- energy
- water
- compute
- materials
- governance
- health
- logistics
- geography
- evidence status
- project/program
- infrastructure node
- timeline/provenance

The same underlying corpus can therefore be rendered as a 12×12 lattice, project map, evidence map, provenance graph, timeline, geographic map, or infrastructure topology without duplicating source files.

## Machine-readable direction

Alexandria 2.0 is being designed around four durable layers:

1. **Source vault** — immutable/versioned originals.
2. **Provenance registry** — identity, URI, hash/commit, timestamps, rights, review and release state.
3. **Knowledge graph** — typed entities, claims, evidence and relationships.
4. **Retrieval/presentation** — semantic retrieval, graph query, public pages and 12×12 rendering.

A validation plane spans all four layers for schema validation, dimensional checks, citation integrity, contradiction tracking, quarantine enforcement, and graph invariants.

## Canonical graph objects

At minimum Alexandria should treat these as first-class objects:

- `Artifact`
- `Claim`
- `Evidence`
- `Quantity`
- `Person/Agent/Organization`
- `Program/Project`
- `Repository/Source`
- `House/Sphere`
- `InfrastructureNode`
- `Location`
- `Revision`
- `ReleaseState`

Claims, not entire documents, are the primary unit of evidence status.

## Contribution principle

Contribute where the source lives. Cross-project work should link repositories and preserve provenance rather than copying trees into a monolith.

Alexandria is an index, graph, and public canon over a federation of repositories — not a reason to erase their independent histories.
